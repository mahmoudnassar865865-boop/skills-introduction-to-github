# Research Paragraph

Research affects company decisions because it helps the company
understand the market, users, and available opportunities before building any product or service.
Market research shows competitors and market needs, while user research explains users’ problems, behaviors, and expectations.
Data analytics helps the company use real data to support decisions instead of relying only on assumptions.
For Ecolyro, research is important because psychology and tourism users have different needs, so the company must understand each target group before making product or business decisions.
